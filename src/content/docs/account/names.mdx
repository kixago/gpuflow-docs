GPUFlow keeps the name you use as a renter apart from the name you use as a provider, so nobody can tell the two belong to the same person. Your email address and your real name are never shown. You set all your names in **Account settings → Your names**.

## Username

<Tip id="names.username">

Your username is how you sign in (your email address works too), and it's the name shown next to your reviews unless you turn on a display name. It's %names.usernameMin% to %names.usernameMax% characters long: letters a to z, digits and single dots, underscores or hyphens between them. Capital letters don't make a different name.

</Tip>

- You choose it when you create your account. If you sign up with Google or GitHub, you choose it on the next step.
- Usernames use Latin letters in every language, so everyone can read and type them.

## Display name

<Tip id="names.display">

A display name is optional. Turn it on and it's shown instead of your username next to your reviews. It can be in any language and doesn't have to be unique.

</Tip>

## Provider name and page address

<Tip id="names.provider">

When you list a GPU, renters see your provider name, never your username. Your provider page lives at gpuflow.app/marketplace/provider/ followed by your page address. Every account starts with a random provider name, such as "Quiet Falcon", which you can change whenever you like.

</Tip>

- Your provider name and page address aren't linked to your username or to the reviews you write as a renter.
- Your provider page shows your listings, your provider level and your reviews from renters.

## Changing your names

<Tip id="names.changes">

You can change your username and your page address once every %names.changeDays% days. An old username or page address stays reserved for you for %names.oldNameHoldDays% days, so nobody else can take it, and links to your old page address keep working during that time.

</Tip>

## What's never shown

- Your email address.
- Your real name, including the name on a Google or GitHub account you sign in with.
- Your Google or GitHub profile photo. Everyone gets the same plain icon.
- Which renter name and which provider name belong to the same account.
