## How money reaches you

<Steps>

1. **A rental ends.** The renter pays for the time used. GPUFlow keeps 12% and the other 88% is yours.
2. **Your earnings are held for 7 days.** They show under **Earnings on hold** on **Dashboard → Payments**. If your account is less than 30 days old, the hold is 14 days. The hold protects against card fraud and disputes.
3. **They become ready to cash out.** After the hold they move to **Ready to cash out**, and you get a notification.
4. **You cash out** to your bank through Stripe, our payment partner.

</Steps>

You can also spend your earnings on renting other GPUs. Credits you bought with a card can be spent, but they can't be cashed out.

## Set up payouts (once)

You need a confirmed email address and a payout account with Stripe. Stripe checks your identity and asks for your bank details. It takes a few minutes.

<Steps>

1. Open **Dashboard → Payments**.
2. Under **Cash out**, choose the country where you live or where your business is registered.
3. Click **Set up payouts with Stripe** and follow Stripe's steps. Have an ID and your bank details ready.
4. When you finish, Stripe sends you back to GPUFlow.

</Steps>

<Image src={setup} alt="The Cash out box: the country list and the Set up payouts with Stripe button highlighted" />

Stripe sometimes reviews your details first. That usually takes a few minutes, sometimes a day or two. If a step is still missing, you'll see **Continue setup on Stripe**.

**Countries where payouts work:** United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and the European Economic Area: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden.

## Cash out

<Steps>

1. Open **Dashboard → Payments**.
2. Under **Cash out**, type the amount, or leave the full amount that's filled in.
3. Check the line under it: it shows the $2.50 fee and what you receive.
4. Click **Cash out**.

</Steps>

<Image src={cashout} alt="The Cash out box with the amount field and the Cash out button highlighted" />

- **Minimum:** $25 per cash-out.
- **Fee:** $2.50 per cash-out, no matter the amount. Cashing out less often saves fees.
- The money is sent in US dollars to your Stripe payout account, and Stripe pays it to your bank.
- You get an email and a notification when it's sent. If it doesn't go through, the full amount returns to your balance.
- Change your bank details any time with **Manage payout details on Stripe**.

## If a renter disputes a card payment

Sometimes a renter asks their bank to reverse a card payment. When that happens, GPUFlow takes back earnings from that renter's rentals that are **still on hold**, and you get an email about it. If the dispute is settled in our favor, the earnings come back to you. Earnings that have finished their hold aren't touched.

<Aside type="note">
Your earnings are income. How they're taxed depends on where you live. If you're not sure, ask a tax advisor.
</Aside>
