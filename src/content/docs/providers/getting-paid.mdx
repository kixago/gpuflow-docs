## How money reaches you

<Steps>

1. **A rental ends.** The renter pays for the time used. GPUFlow keeps %fees.platformPercent%% and the other %fees.providerPercent%% is yours.
2. **Your earnings are held for a while.** They show under **Earnings on hold** on **Dashboard → Payments**. The hold protects against card fraud and disputes.
3. **They become ready to cash out.** After the hold they move to **Ready to cash out**, and you get a notification.
4. **You cash out** to your bank account, right from the Payments page.

</Steps>

You can also spend your earnings on renting other GPUs. Credits you bought with a card can be spent, but they can't be cashed out.

### How long the hold is

<Tip id="earnings.hold">

Earnings are held for %earnings.holdDays% days, or %earnings.newAccountHoldDays% days while your account is less than %earnings.newAccountAgeDays% days old. From provider level %earnings.shortHoldLevel%, on an account at least %earnings.shortHoldMinAgeDays% days old, the hold is %earnings.shortHoldDays% days. When both you and the renter have accounts less than %earnings.newPairAgeDays% days old, it's %earnings.newPairHoldDays% days, and a rental GPUFlow checks for possible fraud can be held for up to %earnings.reviewHoldDays% days.

</Tip>

## Set up payouts (once)

You need a confirmed email address, two-factor sign-in turned on, and a payout account. You set it up without leaving GPUFlow: a secure form on the Payments page checks your identity and asks for your bank details. It takes a few minutes.

<Steps>

1. Open **Dashboard → Payments**.
2. Under **Cash out**, choose the country where you live or where your business is registered.
3. Click **Set up payouts**. If you haven't confirmed it's you in the last 10 minutes, you're asked for your two-factor code first.
4. Fill in the form that opens in the box. Have an ID and your bank details ready.
5. Click **Close** when you're done. The box checks your details and shows the result.

</Steps>

<Image src={setup} alt="The Cash out box: the country list and the Set up payouts button highlighted" />

Your details are sometimes checked first. That usually takes a few minutes, sometimes a day or two. If a step is still missing, you'll see **Continue setup**. If more details are needed later, you get an email and a notice in the box with an **Add details** button.

<Aside type="note">
The setup form comes in English, German, Spanish, French, Japanese, Korean, Portuguese and Chinese. In other languages it opens in English; the rest of the page stays in your language.
</Aside>

**Countries where payouts work:** United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Switzerland, and the European Economic Area: Austria, Belgium, Bulgaria, Croatia, Cyprus, Czechia, Denmark, Estonia, Finland, France, Germany, Greece, Hungary, Iceland, Ireland, Italy, Latvia, Liechtenstein, Lithuania, Luxembourg, Malta, Netherlands, Norway, Poland, Portugal, Romania, Slovakia, Slovenia, Spain and Sweden.

## Your bank accounts

Under **Where we send your money** you see the bank accounts and debit cards on file, for example *Chase ••6789*, and which one is the **Default**.

- **Make default** sends future cash-outs to another account.
- **Remove** deletes an account that isn't the default.
- **Add or change a bank account** opens the form in the box.

<Tip id="earnings.newBank">

For your protection, a cash-out to a bank account you just added waits %earnings.newBankHours% hours (your very first bank account doesn't wait). When a bank account is added, you get an email. If it wasn't you, click the link in that email to pause payouts: nothing is sent until our team has checked your account with you.

</Tip>

## Cash out

<Steps>

1. Open **Dashboard → Payments**.
2. Under **Cash out**, type the amount, or leave the full amount that's filled in.
3. Check the line under it: it shows the %earnings.cashoutFeeUsd% fee and what you receive.
4. Click **Cash out**.

</Steps>

<Image src={cashout} alt="The Cash out box with the amount field and the Cash out button highlighted" />

- **Minimum:** %earnings.cashoutMinUsd% per cash-out.
- **Fee:** %earnings.cashoutFeeUsd% per cash-out, no matter the amount. Cashing out less often saves fees.
- The money is sent in US dollars to your payout account and paid out to your bank on the usual schedule.
- You get an email and a notification when it's sent. If it doesn't go through, the full amount returns to your balance.

### When a cash-out is reviewed

<Tip id="earnings.review">

Some cash-outs are checked by our team before they're sent, for example when the same bank account is also used by another GPUFlow account. The cash-out shows **Under review** and you get an email when it's decided. While the check is open, the balances of the accounts involved are on hold: no cash-outs, rentals or top-ups, and earnings don't clear. Rentals already running continue.

</Tip>

## If a renter disputes a card payment

Sometimes a renter asks their bank to reverse a card payment. When that happens, GPUFlow takes back earnings from that renter's rentals that are **still on hold**, and you get an email about it. If the dispute is settled in our favor, the earnings come back to you. Earnings that have finished their hold aren't touched.

<Aside type="note">
Your earnings are income. How they're taxed depends on where you live. If you're not sure, ask a tax advisor.
</Aside>
