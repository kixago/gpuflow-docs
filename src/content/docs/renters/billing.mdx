## Credits

- You pay for rentals with credits. **1 credit = $0.01.**
- You buy credits by card on **Dashboard → Payments → Add credits**. Payment runs through Stripe.
- Each top-up is between **$10 and $500**. There is **no fee**.
- Credits don't expire.

## What happens when you rent

Say you rent a GPU that costs **$0.40 per hour** for **3 hours**.

| When | What happens | Your credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You click **Start 3-hour rental** | $1.20 (3 × $0.40) is **held**. It shows as "In use" on the Payments page. | $1.20 held |
| You use it for 1 hour 30 minutes, then click **End now** | You pay for exactly 1 h 30 min: **$0.60**. | $0.60 comes back |
| Or: you let the 3 hours run out | The rental ends on its own. You pay $1.20. | nothing comes back |

The rules:

- **Billed to the second.** The shortest charge is 1 minute.
- **You never pay more than what was held.**
- **Ending early is free.** Unused time goes straight back to your credits.
- **Need more time?** Click **Add hours** on **Current Rentals** before your time runs out. More credits are held and the same API key keeps working.

## If the GPU goes offline

If the provider's machine stops answering for **10 minutes**, the rental ends by itself. You pay only up to the moment it went offline, and the rest goes back to your credits. You get a notification when this happens.

## Where to see your money

- **Dashboard → Payments** shows what's available, what's held for running rentals, and a history of every top-up, rental and refund.
- **Dashboard → Rental History** lists your finished rentals.
- **The orange button in the header** always shows your available credits. Click it to add more.

## Something went wrong with a rental

On **Current Rentals** or **Rental History**, click **Report a problem** next to the rental. This opens a support ticket with the rental already filled in. Describe what happened, for example:

> Rental #1042 on "RTX 4090 · qwen2.5 7B". From 14:10 to 14:35 every request returned `provider_offline`, but the rental kept running. Please check whether I should be refunded for that time.

We answer by email. If we agree the time wasn't usable, we refund it to your credits.

<Aside type="caution" title="Please talk to us before you dispute a card payment">
If you dispute a GPUFlow payment with your bank, the disputed amount is taken back from your credits and your account is suspended until the dispute is settled. Opening a support ticket first is almost always faster.
</Aside>
